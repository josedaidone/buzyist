Troubleshooting checklist

Work down the list. After each sound, mark heard or not heard on its line in the log; the log keeps the timer state of every attempt, so a pattern shows up (e.g. "silent only while paused", "silent after 3 in a row").

  1. Player stuck? Stop audio, then play the reminder once.
  2. Cooldown per app name? Play the reminder under another app name (lab2), then under buzyist again.
  3. Too many too fast? Play once, wait the time below, play again. Raise the wait until both are heard.
  4. Repeats overlapping? Play ×N with the gap from the header; try 1600 ms, 2500 ms, 4000 ms.
  5. Timer state matters? Play the reminder once in each state. The timer buttons below change the bar's real timer.
  6. Own copy vs stock? Copy the bar's reminder into the app's folder and play the copy.
  7. Is the file itself fine? "Preview on PC" on any sound plays the bar's file on this computer (pick the sample rate that sounds right; probably 44100).
  8. Volume? The header shows the bar's volume; set it below and play again.

The bar's sounds

Stock play works only for shared/ on firmware 27.5; for the others use "Copy & play" (copies the file into /ext/user_assets/<app>/). Length assumes 44.1 kHz mono 16-bit.

Make a sound

Generated here as raw 16-bit PCM, uploaded to the app's folder, then played. Square waves around 2–3 kHz carry best on a small speaker.

To find the bar's sample rate: upload "1 s at 1 kHz" with Rate 44100. If the bar plays about 1 s, it is 44.1 kHz; about 2 s means 22.05 kHz, and so on.
