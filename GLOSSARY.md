# buZyist

A tray app that runs focus timers for Todoist tasks (or anything typed in) on a BUSY Bar or on the PC, and blocks distractions while they run.

## Timer

**Pomodoro**:
One block of focused work, the app's unit of focus. pt-BR: _pomodoro_.
_Avoid_: Tomato, tomate, round (in what people read)

**Session**:
What gets started on a task or a name: one or more pomodoros with breaks between them. pt-BR: _sessão_.
_Avoid_: Pomodoro (for the whole run)

**Break**:
The rest between two pomodoros. pt-BR: _intervalo_.
_Avoid_: Rest (in what people read; the bar's firmware still shows "REST"), pausa

**Pause**:
Stopping the clock by hand, in a pomodoro or a break, until it is resumed. pt-BR: _pausar_ / _pausado_.
_Avoid_: Using it for a break

## Languages

**App language**:
The language buZyist shows its text in, chosen in Settings; English until someone picks another. The extension follows it.
_Avoid_: Locale, Windows language
